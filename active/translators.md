# Translators Team

The Translators Team coordinates the translation of Django and its documentation into languages other than English.

## Scope of responsibilities

Translation work happens on [Transifex](https://app.transifex.com/django/django/), organized into a per-language team with its own coordinator. The Translators Team is the group of language coordinators, together with anyone helping run the overall translation process.

Team members will:

- Coordinate their language team's translation work on [Transifex](https://app.transifex.com/django/django/), including reviewing and approving translations from contributors.
- Review requests for new language teams and approve or reject them.
- Help contributors get started per the [localizing Django guide](https://docs.djangoproject.com/en/dev/internals/contributing/localizing/).
- Answer questions and discuss translation issues on the [Django internationalization forum](https://forum.djangoproject.com/c/internals/i18n/14).
- Coordinate with [Releasers](./releasers.md) so translations are pulled from Transifex into the repository for releases.
- Periodically reassess the translation tooling and workflow, and suggest changes where improvements are found.

## Future membership

Anyone can contribute translations by joining a language team on Transifex; this does not require joining the Translators Team itself.

Becoming a language coordinator, and thereby a member of the Translators Team, works as follows:

- A contributor signs up on [Transifex](https://app.transifex.com/django/django/) and joins or requests a language team.
- An existing coordinator for that language approves the request, or, if the language has no coordinator, an existing Translators Team member does.
- Each year, every non-Fellow member will need to reaffirm their membership with the team.
